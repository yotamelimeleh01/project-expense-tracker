    Everything in the box

    Ten jobs, one app, no add‑ons

    Nothing here is an upsell. Every project you create gets all of it.

    Receipts that type themselves

    Photograph the receipt and FlipSmart reads the amount, the date and the vendor, guesses the phase from the vendor name, and matches it to a contractor you already know. All on the device — the image never goes to a third‑party OCR service.

    • Tesseract OCR in the browser
    • Photos compressed to ~200 KB
    • Multiple photos per expense

    One honest all‑in number

    Partner cash out of pocket plus loan principal funded at closing. Draws are reimbursements, so they are never double‑counted. It is on screen on every tab, because it is the only number that decides whether the deal works.

    • Split four ways: buy, build, hold, sell
    • Projected profit turns into final profit
    • Cash deals hide every loan field

    Budget vs actual, per phase

    Set a budget line per phase, or leave it blank and it is tracked but never flagged. Pick your own variance threshold. The dashboard turns red before the deal does.

    • On budget · watch · over budget
    • Overrun projects surface on the portfolio
    • Rename, add or delete phases per project

    A profit waterfall that ends arguments

    Capital back first, then preferred return accrued per dollar from the day it was spent, then upside by equity. Every partner sees the same table, and it always balances to the proceeds.

    • Handles shortfalls proportionally
    • Flags unassigned spend
    • Partners need not have logins

    A schedule that knows what matters

    Start from a typical twelve‑phase rehab, then bend it to your job. FlipSmart runs a forward and backward pass, finds the critical path, and tells you what a slip actually costs in holding money per day.

    • Dependencies, slack and float
    • Circular dependencies caught by name
    • Live Gantt with a today line

    Draw requests the lender accepts

    Every reimbursable expense since your last draw, pre‑ticked. Untick what will not fly, then print a properly itemised request with property, borrower, loan position, subtotals, certification and signature line.

    • Warns if you exceed the holdback
    • Warns on lines with no receipt
    • Prints or saves as PDF

    Crew records that keep you clean

    One directory across every project. W‑9 on file, insurance certificate expiry, licence expiry. FlipSmart warns you thirty days out and totals labour and services per contractor per year for 1099s.

    • $600 threshold flagged automatically
    • Materials correctly excluded
    • No full tax IDs stored

    The paperwork, filed

    Contract, ALTA settlement statement, deed, loan docs, insurance, permits, inspections, bids, lien releases, listing. Eleven slots plus your own. And the settlement statement can be read straight into closing costs.

    • Private storage, signed links
    • PDF, images and Office files
    • ALTA and Closing Disclosure parsing

    Share exactly as much as you mean to

    Owner, editor and viewer roles for people with accounts — enforced by the database, not by hiding buttons. For lenders and investors, a read‑only link with four switches and an expiry date.

    • Show or hide ledger, splits, budget, schedule
    • 7 days, 30 days or never
    • Revoking takes effect at once

    It works where the work is

    Basements, new builds with no drywall, rural lots. FlipSmart keeps writing to the phone and replays the queue oldest‑first when signal comes back. Install it to the home screen and it opens like an app.

    • Durable offline write queue
    • Last good data cached per user
    • Installable on iOS and Android

    No hand‑waving

    The three calculations everything else hangs off

    1 All‑in

    Partner cash out of pocket + Loan principal funded at closing = Total all‑in

    Draws are deliberately absent. A draw hands back money already spent and already counted — adding it again is the single most common way a flip's profit gets overstated.

    2 Profit

    Sale price − Total all‑in = Profit

    Labelled projected until you mark the deal sold, then it is simply the profit. Colour‑coded, so a deal going underwater is obvious from the portfolio screen.

    3 The waterfall

    Capital back, pro rata if short ↓ Preferred return, accrued per dollar per day ↓ Upside, split by equity

    If nobody set equity percentages, upside falls back to the proportion each partner actually funded. Equity over 100% is rejected before it can poison a distribution.

    Under the hood

    Boring where it counts

    Permissions live in the database

    Row level security decides what every query is allowed to return. A viewer cannot write, and a stranger cannot read, even with the browser console open.

    Receipts and documents are private

    Files sit in private buckets and are only ever handed out as short‑lived signed links, checked against your membership of that project.

    Receipt reading never leaves the phone

    The OCR engine runs in your browser. No receipt image is shipped to an outside service to be read.

    Losing signal is not losing data

    Only network failures queue. A permission or validation error tells you immediately, instead of silently pretending it saved.

    No tax identity numbers stored

    W‑9 status is a yes or no with an optional last four digits. FlipSmart does not want your subs' full identifiers and does not keep them.

    Your data leaves with you

    Export the ledger to CSV whenever you like, print anything to PDF, and sign out to wipe the cached copy off that device.

    Straight answers

    Questions people actually ask

    Do I need a construction loan to use it?

    No. Flip a project to a cash deal and every loan field, the draw tracker and the payoff panel disappear. All‑in becomes simply what the partners spent.

    Can my partner see the deal without me sending screenshots?

    Yes. Invite them by email as an editor or a viewer. If they should not have an account at all — a lender, say — generate a read‑only link and choose which four things it reveals.

    What happens if I add an expense with no signal?

    It saves on the phone and shows in your totals right away. A bar at the top tells you exactly how many changes are waiting, and they upload in the correct order as soon as you have a connection.

    Is the receipt scanner accurate enough to trust?

    It gets you most of the way and shows its confidence, and every field stays editable. Treat it as fast typing rather than magic — you glance, correct if needed, and save.

    How is a preferred return calculated?

    Per dollar, per day. Each contribution accrues from the date it was actually spent through to the sale date, so a partner who funded in March is not treated the same as one who funded in September.

    Can I change the phase list?

    Yes, per project. Rename them, add trades you use, delete the ones you never touch, and set a default cost type for each so new expenses come pre‑filled.

    Does it work on a phone?

    It is built for one. Add it to your home screen and it opens full screen with no browser bars, holds its own cache, and runs offline.

    What does it cost?

    Creating an account is free and there is no card required. Unlimited projects, partners and receipts.
